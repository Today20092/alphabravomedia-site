## Ongoing Podcast Production for Lavena Wellness

Lavena Wellness is building a YouTube presence around education, trust, and long-term patient value. The brand connects to Lavena Health's Temple Terrace clinic and its online wellness platform, with physician-led care around medical weight loss, GLP-1 treatment, peptides, hormones, longevity, and primary care.

Alpha Bravo Media is supporting the project as an ongoing podcast and channel management partner. That includes recording and editing podcast episodes, creating shorts from the strongest moments, and helping package the channel so the content can keep working after each episode is published.

The goal is not just to publish isolated videos. It is to turn the YouTube channel into a useful education library where current patients, future patients, and the broader Tampa Bay community can learn directly from real physicians instead of guessing through low-quality health content online.

## Production Workflow

The production is designed around a repeatable, professional setup that can support continued publishing. The workflow includes:

- Multi-camera podcast recording with Lumix camera coverage
- Shure MV7+ microphones for clean, broadcast-style dialogue
- Microphone pre-amplifiers for stronger gain staging and lower noise
- AI noise reduction and post-production audio cleanup
- Multi-cam edits that keep the conversations moving naturally
- Full podcast exports for YouTube
- Short-form clips for YouTube Shorts and other social platforms
- Channel management support for a consistent publishing presence

## Channel Growth and Education Strategy

For Lavena Wellness, the content strategy is centered on clarity, trust, and medical authority. Each podcast episode gives the doctors another opportunity to answer common questions, explain sensitive topics respectfully, and create educational material that patients can return to over time.

The short-form clips help make the full conversations easier to discover, while the long-form podcast builds a deeper content library. Together, the goal is to grow the brand, educate viewers, and support future business by making the YouTube channel a reliable source of information for weight loss, wellness, hormones, and preventive health.

Lavena Health also has a local clinic presence in Temple Terrace, Florida, which supports the trust behind the content. Viewers can learn more through the [Lavena Health website](https://lavenahealth.com/home) or the [local Google Maps listing](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3PRERqdTf3qMtJYw6).
