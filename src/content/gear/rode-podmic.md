I use the RØDE PodMic now. I like the all-metal build and the quality of the microphone.

## Why I use it

My approach is to capture good audio with the polar pattern I need, then shape the sound with EQ afterward. The PodMic is a dynamic microphone with a cardioid pickup pattern.

For podcasts, I do not think you need a very expensive dynamic microphone. I would put the time into learning EQ and getting a good recording first.

## Before you buy

This is the **XLR PodMic**, not the PodMic USB. It needs an XLR cable and a compatible audio interface or recorder, such as the [Zoom F8n Pro](/gear/zoom-f8n-pro/).

See [RØDE's specifications](https://edge.rode.com/pdf/products/108/PodMic_Datasheet_07_FA.pdf) for the cardioid pattern, dynamic capsule, and metal construction.
