## Compact Lumix Battery Charging
This Tilta DMW-BLK22 Portable Charging Station is a useful way to keep Lumix batteries organized and charged without bringing a larger charging setup to every shoot.

## Charges Four Batteries
It can charge up to four DMW-BLK22 batteries at the same time, which is helpful for long production days when camera batteries are rotating between the bag, the charger, and the camera.

## USB-C PD Power
The USB-C interface supports up to 45W power input, so it works well with modern USB-C wall chargers, power stations, and other PD power sources I already keep in the kit.

## Built For Lumix Shooters
It is designed for Panasonic, Tilta, and many third-party DMW-BLK22 batteries, including batteries used with Lumix S5, S5II, S5IIX, S1RII, GH6, GH5II, and G9M2 camera bodies.

## Recommended
If you shoot Lumix and want a small charger that can handle multiple DMW-BLK22 batteries at once, this is a practical add to the bag.
