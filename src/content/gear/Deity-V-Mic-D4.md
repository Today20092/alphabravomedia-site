I use this microphone all the time, it works, sounds good, extremely affordable, and has great wind reduction.

This is a wired, on-camera microphone. It connects to the camera's microphone input and uses plug-in power rather than a rechargeable battery.

## Why I Use It

- Compact on-camera setup
- Included windscreen
- No separate battery to charge
- Auxiliary 3.5mm input for a second audio source

## Best For

Vlogs and lightweight video setups. Check that your camera supplies plug-in power through its 3.5mm microphone input.

See [Deity's product specifications](https://deitymic.com/products/v-mic-d4-mini/) for connection and power requirements. This is not a wireless microphone or a standalone backup recorder.
