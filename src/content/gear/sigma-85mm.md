The Sigma 85mm f/1.4 is the portrait lens in my current kit. Alongside my [Sigma 16–28mm f/2.8](/gear/sigma-16-28mm/), these are the two lenses I currently use.

My kit is used for vlogs, recording video, portraits, and event photography. The 85mm gives me a longer focal length than the wide-angle zoom.

## Before you buy

The affiliate link is for the DG DN Art **L-Mount** model. Confirm that it matches your camera. An 85mm prime has a fixed focal length, so it serves a different role from a wide-angle vlogging lens.
