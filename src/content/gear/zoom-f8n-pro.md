I use the Zoom F8n Pro now for recording 32-bit float audio. I also use it as an audio interface for TikTok Live and for recording videos, so it handles both recording and live audio in my setup.

## High gain for dynamic microphones

The high gain and low-noise preamps are another reason I like this recorder for dynamic microphones, including my [RØDE PodMic](/gear/rode-podmic/).

Zoom specifies **up to 75 dB of microphone input gain** and **equivalent input noise of −127 dBu or less**, measured A-weighted at +75 dB gain with a 150 Ω input. That gives it plenty of gain for low-output dynamic microphones while keeping preamp noise low.

## My USB-C connection

The recorder has a **USB Mini-B** port. I use these [USB-C to Mini-USB adapters](/gear/usb-c-mini-usb-adapters/) to connect it with my USB-C setup.

The USB connection carries data; it does not power the recorder. Use a data-capable cable and power the F8n Pro separately. Audio-interface formats depend on the selected mode and connected device; 32-bit float recording does not mean every livestream receives 32-bit float audio.

See the [Zoom F8n Pro manual](https://zoomcorp.com/manuals/f8n_pro-en/) for connections, power, and audio-interface modes.
