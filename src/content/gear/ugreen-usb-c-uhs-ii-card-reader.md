I use this UGREEN card reader to transfer photos and videos from my Lumix camera directly to my phone.

## My phone transfer workflow

I use it for JPEG photos and lightweight MP4 videos that already have the camera's Real Time LUT baked in. That gives me files I can move to my phone without first bringing them through the computer.

## Before you buy

Check that your phone supports USB-C storage accessories and the file formats you plan to transfer. The reader moves the files; it does not convert them or apply a LUT afterward.
