I bought this UGREEN charger from a friend, and we use it together when traveling. I like the charging speed and the size when we have several devices to charge.

## Why it is in my travel kit

We use it for camera batteries, cameras, DJI Osmo gear, and other devices. We also use it with the Tilta battery charging stations listed on this page.

The screen is a nice extra. It is not something I need, but I like being able to see that a device is charging.

## Match the charger to the device

Check the power requirements of your battery charging station, the output available from the selected port, and your cable. A charger's headline wattage is not a promise that every connected device gets that much power at once.

The [Tilta DMW-BLK22 station](/gear/tilta-dmw-blk22-portable-charging-station/) is for compatible Lumix batteries; the [Tilta NP-FZ100 station](/gear/tilta-np-fz100-portable-charging-station/) is for Sony batteries.
