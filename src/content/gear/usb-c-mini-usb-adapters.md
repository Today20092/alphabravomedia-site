I use these adapters to connect my [Zoom F8n Pro](/gear/zoom-f8n-pro/) with my USB-C setup. They have a USB-C female connection and a Mini-USB male connector.

## Get the right connector

The F8n Pro uses **Mini-B**, which is different from Micro-B. This link is for the Mini-USB adapters I use.

Use a USB-C cable that supports data transfer. The adapter connects the USB data port; it does not make the recorder run on USB power.

Zoom identifies the port and its power limitation in the [F8n Pro manual](https://zoomcorp.com/manuals/f8n_pro-en/).
