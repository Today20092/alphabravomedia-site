I use this UGREEN USB-C hub with both my phone and my computer. It lets me connect multiple devices when one USB-C port is not enough.

My computer only has one USB-C port, so the hub gives me room to connect the other devices I need without constantly swapping cables.

## Before you buy

USB-C ports do not all support the same functions. Check your phone or computer's support for connected accessories and video output before choosing a hub. The HDMI capability depends on the host device as well as the hub.
