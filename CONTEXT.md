# Alpha Bravo Media gear

The gear guide helps visitors understand the creator's equipment and choose items through affiliate links.

## Language

**Current gear**:
Equipment the creator currently owns and uses.
_Avoid_: Recommendations when describing ownership

**Alternative**:
Equipment suggested as another option, listed separately from current gear. An alternative does not imply firsthand use.

**Kit**:
A group of equipment selected for a specific production task.

**Personal note**:
The creator's firsthand explanation of how an item is used and who it suits.

**Affiliate link**:
A retailer link through which qualifying purchases may earn the creator a commission.
