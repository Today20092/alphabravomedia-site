## Question

Which YouTube channel is the site supporting, who is its audience, and what should visitors learn here?

Recommended direction: introduce the creator, help visitors find relevant videos, and retain useful articles or guides. Remove service-selling copy and lead-generation prompts.

The repository config points to https://youtube.com/@alphabravomedia. Confirm this remains the intended channel. The owner has been asked for main topics and existing content they want to keep. No answer has been assumed.

Repository inventory found four blog articles, fifteen gear articles, twelve portfolio entries with videos, and two galleries. Suggested navigation is Home, Videos, Articles, Gear, and About.
